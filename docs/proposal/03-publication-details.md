# Publication Details

## Project Description

**Working title:** *The Logic of Logos: Variable Consequence and the Limits of Formalization*

**Genre:** Scholarly monograph

**Target length:** 100–200 pages of dense formal and metalinguistic argument, organized as a
multi-part monograph. The target register is *Undecidable Theories* (Tarski, Mostowski, and
Robinson, 1953) — compact, technically precise, aimed at a specialist and advanced-graduate
readership. If the material demands extended treatment, the model expands to Derrida's
*Introduction to Edmund Husserl's Origin of Geometry* (1962): a sustained scholarly
engagement that functions simultaneously as apparatus, argument, and independent
contribution.

## Publication Model

The author is the funding principal. The publication model is self-publication or hybrid
publication, with the author retaining full intellectual and commercial control. Arrangements
with publishers, distributors, and production services are service contracts. The author is
not seeking an advance, a royalty structure, or an employment relationship with any
publisher. The agent's role is to identify, evaluate, and negotiate appropriate service
providers on the author's behalf.

## Services Sought

The author holds a large corpus of existing material: hundreds to thousands of pages of
notes, sketches, partial drafts, and attempted manuscripts accumulated over more than two
decades of active research. The primary production challenge is not generation but
**selection, arrangement, and distillation** — reducing a body of material that could
support many distinct works into a focused monograph without losing the structural
argument that gives the project its coherence.

The author is seeking:

- **Developmental editors** with competence in formal logic, philosophy of mathematics,
  or foundations of physics who can engage with the source material at the level of
  argument, not only prose.
- **Collaborative writers or editorial compilers** who can work through the existing
  corpus, identify the passages and arguments that serve the monograph's through-line,
  and assist in shaping them into publishable form.

All such arrangements are to be structured as written service contracts.

## Technical Infrastructure

A DocBook 5 XML authoring environment with a modular, atomized file structure is already
in place in the project repository, along with a planned XSLT pipeline for LaTeX and XHTML
output. The monograph will be typeset in a format appropriate for formal mathematical
content. Any engaged editorial or production service should be prepared to work within
or alongside this infrastructure, or to propose a contractually specified alternative.

## Rights

The author's default position is retention of exclusive rights to all original writing and
discoveries. Where joint works with past collaborators exist — including work developed
under informal or tacit collaborative arrangements — the author will seek to establish and
secure the relevant exclusive rights in writing as a precondition of publication.

The author is open to structured rights-sharing arrangements with future collaborators,
provided the scope, attribution, and compensation terms are established in written contracts
before the collaborative work begins. No rights are granted by default to any editorial,
production, or distribution service provider; all such parties operate on a work-for-hire
or equivalent basis unless a separate written agreement specifies otherwise.
